Privacy Policy for Focus3

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Focus3 ("the App"), developed and published by Ramy Gomaa Salmy Khamis, collects, uses, and protects user information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Focus3 does not require account registration and does not collect your name, email address, or personal files. All tasks, timers, and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising Services

To keep the app free and allow unlocking premium themes, Focus3 integrates the Start.io advertising SDK. Start.io may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, device model, and general location data to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.

For more details, please review the Start.io Privacy Policy.

3. Data Security and User Rights

We value your privacy and do not sell personal data. You can reset or limit ad tracking anytime through your Android device settings.
